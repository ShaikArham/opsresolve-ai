OpsResolve AI

Enterprise support intelligence portfolio project by Shaik Arham Saquib.

This workspace uses 200 synthetic incidents across 12 systems, six support teams, 10 agents and 50 fictional reporters. The 26 SOPs and ticket histories link to the same validated dataset.

What is connected

Incident browsing, status and priority filters, SLA calculations, recorded resolutions, event histories and published procedures.

Experimental analysis

A text classifier trained on 105 historical cases suggests categories. SOP matching and historical-ticket retrieval provide cited evidence. Priority follows an impact-and-urgency matrix. The model asks for evidence when matches are weak or categories conflict.

Analysis runs when you select Analyze incident or use the triage lab. Human review decisions last for this page session and do not update the incident records.

All SLA clocks use elapsed UTC time with 24-hour support coverage. Pending time is included. The workspace is fixed at 30 September 2026, 17:00 UTC.

The sample wording is templated. It supports a working demonstration and initial implementation tests. It does not establish production AI accuracy.

Dataset v1.1 · All people, systems and incidents are fictional · Historical outcomes and AI recommendations are distinct.
